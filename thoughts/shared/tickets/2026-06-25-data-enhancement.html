OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company that conducts research and implements machine learning. The research organization is dedicated to advancing artificial intelligence safely and beneficially. OpenAI aims to advance artificial general intelligence (AGI) that is safe, beneficial, and aligned with human values. The company focuses on

creating AI systems that are capable of performing a wide range of tasks and learning autonomously from diverse data.
